Remember when we used to subscribe to YouTube channels? Getting dropped into YouTube these days is often a mix of our subscriptions and what the algorithm wants us to see. While this is great for discovery, it also demands more of our attention, making it harder to curate what we want to watch. I built a [newsletter](https://github.com/erycwang/youtube-digest) to solve that!

The further integration of Shorts into the YouTube app, while ultimately entertaining, keeps me scrolling. In an effort to balance my digital hygiene, I've been using the [Brick app](https://getbrick.com/). Newsletters have been helpful in keeping me up to date in the morning while providing a finite ask on my attention. I plan on using this aggregation of videos when I have some downtime and want to watch some videos, without being dropped into the homepage.

### A few things I learned building this

- GitHub Actions is an easy way to orchestrate and run recurring tasks
- Simple agent creation is OOTB in March of 2026
- It's really easy to get rate limited by Claude :(
